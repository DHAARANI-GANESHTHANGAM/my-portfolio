About Me

My story in a nutshell ;)

Hey, I'm Dhaarani — a developer who likes the part where an idea stops being a notebook and starts being something people can open in a browser.

Most of my recent work is AI applications. The one I\u2019m proudest of is a dementia care platform \u2014 an Android app over a Flask API where voice assessments, speech analysis and a PubMedBERT retrieval layer in pgvector do real work for patients and the people caring for them.

Alongside it, PROPOSAI, an RFP generator with a JavaScript front end over a Python reasoning layer, shipped through sixteen production deploys \u2014 and a Slack AI agent in Node that keeps its own memory so a conversation picks up where it left off.

The other half is data: training the small, useful kind of model and actually deploying it — an insurance-cost predictor served from a pickled regressor, a stock-portfolio analysis app, and a long tail of Python experiments that feed the bigger builds.

Common thread: I'd rather have something deployed and imperfect than perfect and local.
